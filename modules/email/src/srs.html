1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose

This document defines the software requirements for the Email Notification module of the Authentication System.

1.2 Scope

Email notification delivery, templating, and audit trail

1.3 Module Dependencies

ModuleDependency TypeDescription
AUTHAsync (RabbitMQ)Receives verification email requests from AUTH module
RECRAsync (RabbitMQ)Receives application, interview, and offer email requests from RECR module
LOGAsync (RabbitMQ)Publishes email delivery events to Logger module

3. Functional Requirements

Req ID Module Requirement Use Case Priority Description
FR-001 EMAIL Send Verification Email UC-01 High System shall send verification emails with OTP codes to users
FR-002 EMAIL Resend Verification Email UC-01 High System shall allow resending verification emails with rate limiting
FR-003 EMAIL Track Email Delivery UC-02 Medium System shall track email delivery status (sent, delivered, failed)
FR-004 EMAIL Log Email Events UC-02 Medium System shall log all email events for audit trail
FR-005 EMAIL Retry Failed Emails UC-03 Medium System shall retry failed email deliveries with exponential backoff
FR-006 EMAIL Send Application Confirmation UC-04 High System shall send confirmation email when candidate submits job application
FR-007 EMAIL Send Interview Invitation UC-05 High System shall send interview invitation email with calendar details
FR-008 EMAIL Send Offer Letter UC-06 High System shall send offer letter email with position and salary details
