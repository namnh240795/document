2.2 Key Design Decisions

#DecisionRationaleResolves
1Use RabbitMQ for async email processing Decouples email sending from request handling, improves reliability NFR-001, NFR-002
2Use SendGrid/SMTP as email provider Reliable email delivery with tracking capabilities NFR-004
3PostgreSQL for email_logs ACID compliance for audit trail, consistent with other modules DR-001
